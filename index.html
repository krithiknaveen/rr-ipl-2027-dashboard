MODEL CARD / TRANSPARENT METHODS

How the rankings are built

Historical pressure scores are a separate leaderboard. RR candidate order is determined by the frozen speculative WatchIndex, which combines adjusted historical quality with planning assumptions.

01 / MATCH CONTEXTMeasure pressure

Win-probability leverage and chase pressure; a distinct RR stress proxy informs the historical forecast.

02 / PLAYER EFFECTEstimate residuals

Batting and bowling outcomes compared to state-conditioned baselines; shrink thin samples.

03 / RR FITApply role priorities

Blend historical Q with role fit; adjust for sample transfer, age, recency, fitness and hypothetical bid.

04 / AUCTION ACCESSApply gates & P

Use WatchIndex for scouting; use ConditionalIndex only once release, registration and clearance are assumed.

Two pressure definitions
Do not substitute one for the other

0.73 × leverage + 0.18 × chase tension
+ 0.07 × collapse/death + 0.02 × knockout

Top quartile of model-eligible deliveries is labelled high pressure. Historical Round 1 rankings use a distinct 50/25/15/10 component score.

RR stress proxy =
0.55 × chase closeness + 0.34 × phase/collapse
+ 0.08 × wickets/7 + 0.03 × knockout

The RR model uses within-season/format top-quartile stress and a shrunk pressure-uplift term, selected weight 0.08.

Fairness by competition

IPL events are weighted 1.00, T20Is 0.25 and emerging formats 0.08. A further 0.70 factor applies to weaker T20I opposition. These are model assumptions.

Sample-size controls

Per-100-event residuals shrink toward neutral. Frozen prior 400; decay 0.70. Pressure differential is capped ±35 before further shrinkage.

Player gates

Scenario eligibility is not proof of auction registration. A contract-holder requires release; every candidate requires registration, eligibility and clearance.

Extras reconstruction

`extras1_runs` is an event-type code, not the extras amount. Only 128 of 406 recorded winning margins match the minimum-extras reconstruction exactly. Pressure labels may be wrong.

Joint rank fragility

With player-specific simultaneous perturbations, the average original Top-10 overlap falls to 6.10/10 (moderate) or 5.29/10 (broad). These are illustrative scenarios, not confidence intervals.

Auction reality

Retention, release, registration, eligibility, bidding price and overseas constraints are unresolved. Listed candidates are not confirmed available at the 2027 auction.
